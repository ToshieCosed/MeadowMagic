# Strata ↔ Minecraft 1.21 block parity

Compared against the 1.21.4 block list (975 blocks, not counting technical and wall-mounted variants). Generated from the game data and Minecraft's blockstates.

|---|---|
| In Strata | 333 |

| Missing | 607 |

A resource pack re-textures almost everything in the first two rows. Chests, cocoa pods and torches keep Strata's look, because Minecraft draws those from 3D models rather than plain block faces. Strata blocks with no Minecraft counterpart (tin, bauxite, titanium and platinum ores, metal bricks and chests, Dusk and pocket blocks) also keep their own look, and any texture in a pack for a block Strata doesn't have is skipped.

## Good next additions

These fit systems Strata already has, so they are cheaper than their count suggests.

1. **Bamboo**: the plant, bamboo block, planks and mosaic, plus the full wood family. The wood builders added in v0.14.0 make this mostly data.
2. **Remaining flowers**: azure bluet, red and white tulips, lily of the valley, sunflower, lilac, rose bush, peony, torchflower and pink petals. Each one is a sprite, and bone meal on grass would spread them.
3. **Simple naturals**: hay bale, dried kelp block, coarse dirt, dirt path (shovel on grass), rooted dirt, moss and azalea.
4. **Walls and buttons** for every stone and wood. Walls come from the same shape builder as slabs, and buttons plug into the activation sensors that doors and pressure plates use.
5. **The 16-colour sets**: concrete, concrete powder, glazed terracotta, the rest of terracotta, glass panes, beds and candles. They build on the dye system.
6. **Stone variants**: cracked and chiseled stone bricks, chiseled, cut and smooth sandstone, and chiseled tuff and deepslate.
7. **Workstations**: stonecutter, composter (crops to bone meal), campfire (slow cooking), barrel, cauldron and anvil.
8. **Caves**: glow lichen, dripstone, amethyst (could stand in for Dusk crystals), and sculk later.
9. **Copper blocks** with oxidation, waxing and scraping.
10. **Redstone basics**: lever, button, dust and lamp, wired into the activation sensors.

Items worth adding next: apples (oak and dark oak leaves), golden apples and carrots, glistering melon, glow berries, suspicious and rabbit stew, poisonous potato, honey, leads, name tags, compass, clock and spyglass.
